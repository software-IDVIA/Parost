Frequently Asked Questions

What do the alert colors mean? +

The red color indicates an Excessive alert: the vibration has exceeded the critical threshold of 1.5g.

The neutral color means that the state is Acceptable and the levels remain below the risk threshold.

How do the bridge sensors work? +

The sensors collect real-time data on vibration. This information is processed automatically to generate alerts on your dashboard.

How do I receive alert notifications on the Apple Watch? +

Your Apple Watch is synchronized to warn you of any structural anomalies detected by the sensors.

Can I check data offline? +

Both real-time visualization and alert synchronization require an internet connection.
